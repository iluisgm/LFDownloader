Welcome to LFDownloader

Videos, music and streams — caught automatically while you browse, saved in the quality you pick.

Play anything

Open a page and start the video or song. LFDownloader listens to what the page loads.

Pick a quality

The toolbar badge shows what was found. Choose 1080p, 720p, audio only…

Right-click to scan

Right-click any video or page → Scan page for media, or use the Download button over videos.

One more step. Allow LFDownloader to read media requests on websites. Without this it can't see any videos.

Shortcuts: Alt + Shift + D opens LFDownloader · Alt + Shift + S scans the page
